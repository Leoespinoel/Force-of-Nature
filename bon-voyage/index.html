Est. Mauritius

Not just a tee

Wear it like you Mean it.

Look good. Feel good. Do good.

These aren't trade-offs. They're intertwined, one habit built into the next. Look good, and you feel it. Feel good, and you want to keep doing something that matters. Do good, and it's no longer just yours, it rubs off. A friend notices. They try it too. That's how a movement actually starts, not from a mission statement, but from people quietly passing something good along. It's all contagious.

Who's with us

The podcast hands the mic to the people doing the work, reef to forest floor. The community is everyone who wears it like they mean it, Mauritius outward. Our partnerships put the money where the work is, and the impact is counted, not claimed.

What we make

Design starts with a story worth telling, not a print worth selling. Production stays small and answerable — cut, sewn, and shipped plastic-free. Find the drop in the store, or contact us to build, stock, or ask the harder question.
